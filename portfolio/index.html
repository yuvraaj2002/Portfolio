04 — HOW I WORK

Operating rules
I ship with.

A model is one part of the system.
The rest is engineering.

My work connects models to workflows: structured extraction from patents and documents, multi-agent voice ops, and shared LLM access across services.

I care about the boundaries—what gets validated, what happens when a provider fails, and how a human checks the result when the cost of a mistake is high.

01

Structured outputs and evals.

Typed contracts, checked schemas, and measured quality—not free-form hope that the model “got it.”

02

Cost and latency first-class.

Model choice, caching, and batching belong in the design, not as an afterthought once traffic shows up.

03

Human review where mistakes are costly.

Automate when error cost is low; keep a person in the loop when it isn’t—IP, healthcare, and legal included.
