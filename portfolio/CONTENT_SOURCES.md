# Public content policy

The portfolio shows high-level work contexts, contributions, and selected skill keywords. Source documents stay local and are never rendered or copied into the served site.

Implementation methods, architecture, processing sequences, provider combinations, operational settings, benchmarks, private links, and company or client records are excluded. Naming a skill is not permission to describe a proprietary deployment.

MaxVal and Microsoft community-role descriptions come from information supplied by the user. No employment permission or contractual clearance is inferred.
